# NotCooked: product notes

NotCooked is a planner for students. It is built around one question: *what do I need to do right now?* Every screen is designed to make that answer obvious without asking the student to sort through everything else first.

## Who it is for

Students who keep coursework in more than one place and lose time deciding what to start. NotCooked is student-first: it is designed around the student's own workload.

## Design principles

**One clear next step.** Today leads with a single recommended task and shows the reasons for the choice, so the recommendation can be questioned instead of just followed.

**Keep the full list somewhere else.** Long lists are useful, but they are noise when you are trying to start. Schoolwork holds the complete inventory so Today can stay focused on what matters now.

**Nothing arrives unchecked.** Anything imported or scanned goes to a review screen first. Calendar imports are sorted into upcoming work, past-due work, items that already exist and events that are not assignments, so a long feed becomes a short decision. Nothing is added until the student confirms.

**Capture should be fast.** Assignments can be typed, pasted, uploaded or photographed, because homework arrives in all of those forms.

**Undoable by default.** Completing work can be reversed, because mistakes at the end of a long day are common.

**AI is optional.** AI-assisted scanning and planning are opt-in, can be turned off, and have a basic alternative that works without them.

## The three screens

### Today

The immediate view. A **Next Up** card recommends one task and lists why it was chosen, with a button to start a focus session or mark it done. Below it: work due today and work the student planned for today.

### Plan

The dated view. A week and a month layout show how many assignments fall on each day. Assignments without a date, or whose planned date has passed, are surfaced so they can be given a new one.

### Schoolwork

The full inventory. Everything the student owns, including work with no due date, can be viewed by due date, by course or by recent activity. Importing and adding assignments starts here.

## Focus sessions

A focus session pairs a task with a timer length. The student can accept the recommended task, choose another one, or name a custom topic, and pick a session length. The timer keeps the session to one thing until it closes, and the end of a session offers to mark the assignment done.

## Imports

- **School calendar.** Canvas and other school systems can publish a private calendar link. NotCooked reads it on the device and turns calendar items into assignments to review.
- **Google Classroom.** A read-only connection lists assignments from the student's classes to review and add.
- **Text, files and photos.** Pasted text, uploaded documents and photographed worksheets are read into draft assignments to check and edit.

## Status

NotCooked is in private beta. It has been beta-tested with 25+ students and iterated using their feedback.
