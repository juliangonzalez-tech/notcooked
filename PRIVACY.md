# NotCooked: privacy at a glance

This page summarizes how NotCooked handles student data. It is a plain-language overview of the product, not a legal policy. The Privacy Policy and Terms inside the app are the authoritative documents.

## What NotCooked stores

- **Your account.** You sign in with email, Apple or Google. NotCooked keeps the sign-in details needed to identify your account.
- **Your coursework.** The assignments, courses and settings you add or import are stored with your account so they are there when you sign in again.

## What stays on your device

- **Connection credentials.** When you connect Google Classroom, the connection is kept in the device's secure storage, not in NotCooked's database.
- **Your calendar link.** A school calendar link is used on your device to read your calendar. It is not sent to NotCooked's servers.
- **Reminders.** Reminders are scheduled on your device.

## Imports

- Anything imported or scanned is shown to you for review before it is added.
- The original photo or document you scan is not kept after you save the assignments you reviewed.

## AI features

AI-assisted scanning and AI planning are optional.

- You are asked before AI is used, and you can turn each one off. Without AI, NotCooked uses a basic scan and a basic plan.
- NotCooked does not store the text, photos or assignment details you send for AI scanning or planning. It keeps only usage counters used to limit abuse, and basic request information such as sizes and timing, never the content itself.

## Permissions

- **Camera and photo library:** used only when you choose to scan or attach an assignment.
- **Notifications:** used only for the reminders, daily check-in and focus-session alerts you set up.

## Tracking

NotCooked does not include advertising, analytics or tracking software.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your account from Settings in the app. This removes your account and the coursework stored with it, and clears NotCooked's data from your device.

## Beta

NotCooked is in private beta, and this page describes the current beta. It will be updated as the product changes.
